# Fabricated: fixed findings

Every defect found and fixed, by ID: where it was, how it failed and what triggered it, why the tests missed it, and
the fix with how it was verified. Open findings are in [`roadmap.md`](roadmap.md#findings); a finding moves here, in
the same change, once its fix is verified. Code, commits and the other documents cite findings by ID.

Severity: **P0** crash or data loss on a normal launch · **P1** a supported feature silently doesn't work ·
**P2** wrong behaviour or a leak under realistic conditions · **P3** diagnostics, hygiene, unlikely conditions.
