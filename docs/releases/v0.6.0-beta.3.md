# Fabricated v0.6.0-beta.3

> **Beta.** Fabricated runs Fabric mods on NeoForge and Forge. Many Fabric mods work; some still don't. Back up your
> worlds.

The third beta is a compatibility release: no new platform, and far fewer Fabric mods' hooks left out. Where NeoForge
or Forge replaced a piece of vanilla's code that Fabric mods hook, Fabricated now puts vanilla's code back or follows
the hook to where the code went. It covers NeoForge (1.21.1, 1.21.11, 26.1.2, 26.2, 26.3), Forge 61 to 66 (1.21.11,
26.1.2, 26.2, 26.3), Forge 52 (1.21.1) and Forge 47 (1.20.1), as v0.6.0-beta.2 did.

## What changed on every platform

- **Vanilla's calls put back where Fabric mods hook them** (SEAM-1 to SEAM-8). The platforms ask their own extension
  methods where vanilla calls a block's or an item's method, so a Fabric mod's hook at vanilla's call found nothing.
  Where a Fabric mod hooks such a call, and no NeoForge or Forge mod's mixin needs the platform's, vanilla's call is
  back for the blocks and items that keep the platform's default: piston stickiness, block breaking, the harvest
  check, the shears, fishing rod and shield checks, friction, sound types, the piglins' armour check and, on NeoForge,
  enchantment levels. Carpet, FrozenLib, Wilder Wild, Origins, BCLib, BetterNether, Artifacts and Create's Porting Lib
  get hooks back.
- **Hooks follow code the platform moved** (STRUCT-1, STRUCT-2, OVERLOAD-3, OVERLOAD-4, LAMBDA-13). A hook at code
  the platform moved into a method of its own, into a lambda or into one of its hooks is pointed there where
  Fabricated can prove it then does what it does on Fabric, and stays left out otherwise. YUNG's API's and Friends
  and Foes' structure entity processing works on every version (STRUCT-1).
- **Pre-flight reads more as Mixin does** (PRE-29 to PRE-36, VANILLA-5): `instanceof` constants (Lithium's collision
  context), opcode filters, selectors with whitespace in a refmap, locals taken by type, slot or ordinal, and returns
  picked by ordinal. Fewer hooks are left out by mistake.
- **A truer report.** It says what the platform changed about a method or field a hook can't use (PRE-32), and where
  vanilla's own code lacks a hook's point too (PRE-34). A known limit is explained next to the hook it leaves out, with
  what to do, instead of only in these docs. The mixins a mod's own plugin turns down are listed apart from those
  Fabricated left out (LEAVE-4).
- **Less memory at the first launch:** translating the mods allocates 22% to 24% less on Minecraft 26.x, and 4% to 14%
  less on 1.20.1 to 1.21.11 (CI's benchmark, a server with Fabric API and the test mods).

## NeoForge

- **Every version:** with Fabric API, a NeoForge mod's pack that carries both loaders' conditional overlays loads,
  where it stopped the server loading its data packs (PACK-12, issue 400: Tectonic); YUNG's API's hooks into Fabric
  API's pack finder apply (PROVIDER-22); a loot pool a Fabric mod makes with vanilla's constructor is built (LOOT-3).
- **1.21.1:** Fabric HUD layers added in `Gui`'s constructor are drawn (HUD-7: Origins' resource bars); Fabric's load
  conditions are decided before other mods' hooks see the data (COND-1: beside Moonlight's NeoForge build); Origins'
  powers that prevent sleeping, change dropped items and change slipperiness work (LAMBDA-13, OVERLOAD-4, PRE-30);
  FrozenLib's bone meal behaviours run (OVERLOAD-3); JEI's and owo-lib's typed-character hooks apply (LAMBDA-11);
  owo-lib's registry freeze runs on dedicated servers (HOOK-4); Ledger logs the fire an explosion starts (PRE-36).

## Forge 61 to 66 (Minecraft 1.21.11 and 26.x)

- The seam, moved-code and pre-flight fixes above apply as on NeoForge.
- owo-lib's registry freeze runs on dedicated servers (HOOK-4). On Forge 64 to 66, a pack whose `fabric:overlays`
  can't be read keeps its other overlays, with one warning, where the whole pack was left out (PACK-12).

## Forge legacy: Forge 52 (1.21.1) and Forge 47 (1.20.1)

- **Both:** JEI's and owo-lib's typed-character hooks apply (LAMBDA-11); owo-lib's registry freeze runs on dedicated
  servers (HOOK-4).
- **Forge 52:** Origins' powers that change dropped items and slipperiness work (OVERLOAD-4, PRE-30); FrozenLib's bone
  meal behaviours run (OVERLOAD-3); Ledger logs the fire an explosion starts (PRE-36); with Fabric API, a Forge mod's
  item keeps its own crafting remainder (ITEM-2).
- **Forge 47:** more of Fabric mods' interface mixins apply, which Forge 47's older Mixin refuses on its own
  (INTERFACE-2: Origins, owo-lib, Carpet, ServerCore, Wilder Wild); Moonlight's fire hooks apply, so blocks burnt away
  reach Supplementaries and Amendments (STRUCT-2); BCLib's loot pools and BetterNether's bartering table are built
  (LOOT-3); Architectury's screen click and release events fire (LAMBDA-12); Modern Industrialization's generated
  resources reach a world's packs (PRE-31); native mods' mixins are read through their refmaps, so the seam fixes above
  hold beside Forge mods (SEAM-5).

## Support matrix

The loaders, Minecraft versions and tested builds are those of v0.6.0-beta.2, all beta. The matrix gains one row,
Fabric mods' HUD layers added in `Gui`'s constructor (1.21.1, on NeoForge and Forge). See the
[support matrix](https://github.com/cmdralph/Fabricated/blob/main/docs/COMPATIBILITY.md#support-matrix).

## Upgrading from v0.6.0-beta.2

- **Remove the old jar**; two Fabricated jars in `mods` are refused.
- **The first launch translates every mod again**, once, automatically.
- **`config/fabricated.json` carries over:** no setting changed. The developer API is still 1.1.

## Known limits, new in this release's notes

- **Sinking in water** (NeoForge, Forge 52 to 66): the platforms moved vanilla's sinking speed into a method every
  fluid uses, so Additional Entity Attributes' water-speed attribute doesn't change how fast an entity sinks.
- **Reach Entity Attributes on Forge 47:** Forge has its own reach attributes, and the constants the mod changes are
  gone, so a Fabric mod giving reach through it gives none there.
- **Porting Lib on Forge 47** (Create, Farmer's Delight Refabricated): about 45 of its hooks are left out. They port
  hooks Forge's own code makes there.
- **Supplementaries' lumisene on Forge 47:** its fire spread calls a vanilla method Forge replaced, and that call
  fails; lit lumisene burning the blocks around it would stop the server. The report names it.
- **Ledger's campfire logging on 1.21.1** (NeoForge and Forge 52): the platforms' code doesn't keep the value the
  hook takes, so it is left out (LOCAL-6).
- **Members the platforms retyped:** Polymer's entity tracking hooks (NeoForge 1.21.1), Carpet's payload codec hook
  (NeoForge) and Debugify's fix for MC-121706 (NeoForge and Forge) are left out; the report names what changed.
- **Vanilla's calls that can't go back** (the report names each): hooks of a mob's shearing where the platform shears
  it from the shears item (Ledger's and BCLib's sheep and snow golem hooks; NeoForge, Forge 47 to 64), of the map and
  crossbow checks in the held item renderer (Origins' held item transforms), of the held item's block-attack check in
  block breaking, and, from 1.21.11, of a leash's friction and of NeoForge's sweep attack.

The limits of v0.6.0-beta.2's notes still hold, but those fixed here: HUD-7, LOOT-3, and the piston stickiness, block
breaking, item check and structure entity processing hooks those notes list as left out on Forge. All limits are in
[`docs/COMPATIBILITY.md`](https://github.com/cmdralph/Fabricated/blob/main/docs/COMPATIBILITY.md); details of every
change are in [`docs/CHANGELOG.md`](https://github.com/cmdralph/Fabricated/blob/main/docs/CHANGELOG.md).

## Installing

1. Install NeoForge or Forge for your exact Minecraft version.
2. Download the jar for your loader and Minecraft version, from this release or from CurseForge:
   - NeoForge (1.21.1, 1.21.11, 26.1.2, 26.2, 26.3): `fabricated-neoforge-0.6.0-beta.3+mc<minecraft>.jar`.
   - Forge (1.20.1, 1.21.1, 1.21.11, 26.1.2, 26.2, 26.3): `fabricated-forge-0.6.0-beta.3+mc<minecraft>.jar`.
3. Check the jar against `SHA256SUMS.txt`.
4. Put the jar in `mods`, next to your Fabric mods and the official Fabric API jar for that version. Remove Sinytra
   Connector and any older Fabricated jar.

Fabricated never downloads anything and never modifies your mod jars. `logs/fabric-bridge-report.txt` says what loaded
and why anything didn't. If you hit a crash, please open an issue and attach `logs/latest.log` and that report.
